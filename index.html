          Physics sandbox

          The game plays in fullscreen so keys like Ctrl+W can't close it by accident. Hold Esc to leave fullscreen.

          HUD

          X-ray view

          Dynamic shows a 3D x-ray that turns with the ragdoll. Flat shows a still front-view picture of the bones and organs with the same damage. Press X with the x-ray open to switch between them.

          Touch controls

          Touch controls

          Auto turns them on for phones and tablets and off for computers.

          Button layout

          Optimal (recommended): the move stick appears wherever your left thumb lands, fire is under your right thumb with aim, reload, jump and crouch around it, and a second fire button sits top left. Classic: a fixed stick and one fire button. Left-handed: Optimal mirrored.
